⚙️ How MediQ Works

Step 1: Role-based Access

Healthcare professionals log in with secure, role-specific credentials that determine their access levels and permissions within the system.

Step 2: Antibiotic Management

Prescribe, dispense, and track antibiotics with real-time updates on availability, interactions, and appropriate usage guidelines.

Step 3: Analytics & Reporting

Generate comprehensive reports on antibiotic usage patterns, resistance trends, and compliance metrics for informed decision-making.
